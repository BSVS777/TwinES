# Business Brief: TwinES

**Run ID:** 2026-11-19-twines-2000
**Date:** 2026-11-19
**Status:** Analysis Complete (NO-GO 2.45/5, see REPORT.md)

## Idea
**Name:** TwinES (the "ES" honors D.E.S.N., who inspired the original idea)
**Description:** White-label B2B platform for car dealerships that creates a "digital twin" of every vehicle sold: a customer app with a rotating, clickable 3D model of the exact vehicle model showing part-by-part health (green/yellow/orange/red) and AI-predicted next maintenance, plus a workshop panel with AI step-by-step guided repairs and a parts catalog that prioritizes OEM parts while proposing an alternative — all on one shared vehicle history.

## Problem
- Dealerships lose customers to independent workshops once the warranty expires, losing aftersales service and OEM parts revenue.
- Vehicle owners do not know when or what maintenance their car needs; they rely on the dealer/workshop to tell them and react only when something fails.
- Technicians diagnose without a unified vehicle history and without guided procedures, increasing time and errors.
- Dealers want to differentiate through a modern digital customer experience (founder's primary framing; the ROI case most likely rests on aftersales retention).

## Target Audience
**Segment:** B2B2C (dealer pays; vehicle owners and workshop technicians are the end users)
**Customer Profile:** Single- or multi-brand car dealerships in Costa Rica (e.g., Purdy Motors, Veinsa, Grupo Q, Autostar), starting with branches in the San Carlos region (Ciudad Quesada). Note: purchasing decisions for large dealer groups are typically made at headquarters in the Greater Metropolitan Area (GAM).
**Willingness to Pay:** Founder estimate of USD $1–$3 per active vehicle per month (to be validated).

## Business Model
**Type:** SaaS / Subscription
**Details:** Monthly fee per active vehicle registered on the platform, white-labeled with the dealer's brand. Key bet: predictive maintenance alerts convert into workshop appointments and OEM parts sales, justifying the per-vehicle fee. Potential add-ons: onboarding/setup fee for branding and 3D model library.

## Market
**Geographic Focus:** National (Costa Rica), initial regional focus on San Carlos
**Starting Budget:** Bootstrapped (< $5K)

## Known Competitors
None identified by the founder. To investigate: OEM connected-car apps (e.g., MyToyota, Hyundai Bluelink), dealer-built apps, consumer maintenance trackers (e.g., Drivvo, CARFAX Car Care), workshop management software (e.g., Tekmetric, AutoLeap), and repair information providers.

## Additional Context
- Founder has no automotive industry background; the idea arose as an opportunity by analogy with "Symmetry" (gym app with a 3D body model).
- The first milestone (due 2026-12-05) is a documented, partially implemented MVP slice (e.g., vehicle registration → part health view → next-maintenance calculation) delivered with a full QA cycle: test plan, unit tests and automated tests.
- Name note: an earlier name "PurdyPulsES" was dropped to avoid third-party trademark risk and single-dealer lock-in.
